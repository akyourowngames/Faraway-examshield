# EXAMSHIELD - QUESTION PAPER REGISTRY & EVIDENCE MATCHING SYSTEM

## Mission

Build a complete Question Paper Registry system that allows authorized users to upload official examination papers and enables EXAMSHIELD to automatically compare uploaded evidence against registered papers.

This feature must feel production-ready, visually consistent with the existing EXAMSHIELD UI, and fully integrated into the current workflow.
